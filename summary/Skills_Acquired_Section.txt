**Professional Skills:** Strengthened problem-solving abilities debugging complex AI pipelines with non-obvious failure modes, managing JSON parsing inconsistencies, and optimizing computational resource allocation. Improved documentation practices through detailed code commenting, experimental logging, and technical writing for both specialist and general audiences.
