B.5 KEY PERFORMANCE METRICS
================================================================================

Configuration Tested: 19 unique model/retrieval/temperature combinations
Best Performing Setup: Llama 3.3 70B + HyDE + Sentence Chunking (Temp 0.3)

Quantitative Results:
• Question Quality: 91% met rubric standards
• Context Relevance: 88% highly relevant retrievals
• Evaluation Consistency: 94% inter-rater agreement
• Strategy Alignment: 87% dual-strategy consensus
• Processing Time: ~2-3 minutes per question generation
• Retrieval Accuracy: 9.1/10 average relevance score


B.6 COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS
================================================================================

Temperature Impact:
• Temp 0.3: More consistent, standardized questions (preferred for evaluation)
• Temp 0.9: More diverse, creative questions (less rubric adherence)
• Score Difference: 0.7 points average (9.2 vs 8.5 out of 10)

Model Size Impact:
• 8B Models: Faster inference, good quality (9.0/10 average)
• 70B Models: Superior rubric quality, slower inference (9.5/10 average)
• Quality Delta: 0.5 points improvement with 70B models

HyDE Impact:
• With HyDE: 40% more relevant chunks retrieved
• Without HyDE: Faster but less comprehensive context
• Relevance Improvement: +1.5 points (7.6 → 9.1 out of 10)
