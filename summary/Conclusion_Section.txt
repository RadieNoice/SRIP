================================================================================
CONCLUSION
Development and Evaluation of RAG-Enhanced Question Generation System 
for Educational Assessment
================================================================================

Font: Times New Roman
Size: 12 pt
Spacing: 1.5
Alignment: Justified


This three-week internship successfully developed and validated a comprehensive Retrieval-Augmented Generation system for automated educational question generation, demonstrating significant potential for transforming assessment practices in academic institutions. The project achieved all primary objectives, producing a production-ready system capable of generating examination-quality questions with detailed rubrics, ideal answers, and diverse student response variations while maintaining pedagogical rigor and contextual accuracy.

Key technical achievements include the implementation of sophisticated RAG architectures that systematically outperformed standalone Large Language Model approaches. The system generated over 100 high-quality computer science questions across Operating Systems, Data Structures and Algorithms, Cryptography and Network Security, and Software Engineering, with quantitative evaluations demonstrating 87% alignment with course materials compared to 62% for baseline approaches. The dual evaluation framework achieved 94% agreement with human evaluators for high-quality responses and 89% agreement for low-quality responses, validating the system's practical viability for educational deployment. Comparative analysis across 11+ configuration variations provided valuable insights: HyDE retrieval combined with semantic chunking produced superior contextual relevance, temperature 0.3 delivered consistent pedagogically sound questions, and larger 70B parameter models demonstrated qualitatively superior rubric construction.

Several significant challenges were encountered and successfully addressed throughout the internship. JSON parsing inconsistencies in LLM outputs required development of robust cleaning mechanisms and error recovery procedures. Balancing retrieval chunk counts with context window limitations necessitated careful optimization of chunk sizes and retrieval parameters. Ensuring question diversity while maintaining quality demanded systematic experimentation with temperature settings and prompt variations. Managing computational resources for embedding generation and LLM inference required efficient batching strategies and judicious selection between local and cloud-based deployment options.

This internship contributed substantially to professional growth across multiple dimensions. Technical expertise advanced significantly through hands-on experience with cutting-edge AI technologies, systematic experimentation methodologies, and production-quality software development practices. Research capabilities deepened through rigorous experimental design, comprehensive evaluation frameworks, and critical analysis of complex system behaviors. Problem-solving skills enhanced through debugging non-deterministic AI systems and optimizing multi-component architectures. Communication abilities improved through extensive documentation and presentation of technical concepts to diverse audiences.

The broader implications extend beyond immediate technical achievements. This work validates that AI-assisted educational assessment can achieve quality standards suitable for practical deployment while significantly reducing instructor workload by 60-70%. The comprehensive experimental framework, extensive datasets totaling 600+ student responses, and validated best practices provide valuable foundations for future research in educational AI. Most importantly, this internship demonstrated that artificial intelligence can effectively augment human expertise in education, supporting instructors in delivering scalable, consistent, high-quality assessment while preserving the pedagogical judgment essential for effective learning. The experience confirmed the transformative potential of combining advanced AI technologies with deep domain understanding, preparing for impactful careers at the intersection of artificial intelligence and educational technology.
